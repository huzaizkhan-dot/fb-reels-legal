Privacy Policy

FB Reels Scheduler  ·  Effective 19 November 2026

This policy explains what data the FB Reels Scheduler application ("the App") accesses, why, where it is stored, and how it is deleted.

Scope. The App is a private automation tool. It publishes scheduled short-form video posts ("Reels") to Facebook Pages that the operator owns and administers. It is not a public service and has no end users other than the operator.

1. Who operates the App

The App is operated by the owner of the Facebook Pages it publishes to, acting on their own Pages only. It does not act on behalf of, or on behalf of, any other person's Pages.

2. Data the App accesses

DataSourcePurpose
Facebook Page ID and Page nameMeta Graph APITo address the correct Page when publishing
Page access tokenMeta Graph APITo authenticate publishing requests for that Page
Video files, captions, scheduled timesSupplied by the operatorTo compose and schedule posts
Publication result (video ID, permalink)Meta Graph APITo record what was published

3. Data the App does not access

  • It does not collect data about Page followers, viewers, or visitors.
  • It does not read private messages, comments, or profiles.
  • It does not collect analytics about individuals.
  • It does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not sell or rent data to anyone.

4. Where data is stored

  • Credentials (Page access tokens, service-account keys) are stored as encrypted secrets in GitHub Actions. They are never written into source code, spreadsheets, or logs.
  • Scheduling records (slot, Page, caption, status, publisher result) are stored in Google Sheets owned by the operator.
  • Media files exist only transiently on the automation runner while a post is being uploaded, then discarded.

5. Third-party processors

  • Meta Platforms — publishing via the Graph API, governed by the Meta Platform Terms.
  • GitHub — runs the automation and stores encrypted secrets.
  • Google — hosts the operator's spreadsheets and drive.

No other parties receive data.

6. Retention

Page access tokens are retained until revoked. Scheduling records are retained for audit purposes until deleted by the operator. Media files are not retained.

7. Security

Access is limited to a single service account with least-privilege scope, restricted to the operator's own spreadsheets. Tokens are transmitted only over HTTPS to Meta's Graph API. No human-readable copy of any credential is kept in the automation's outputs.

8. Your rights and deletion

Because the App acts only on the operator's own Pages, the data it holds is the operator's own. Deletion is described on the User Data Deletion page, and access can be revoked at any time from Facebook's Settings → Apps and Websites.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children and collects no data from them.

10. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in the effective date above.
